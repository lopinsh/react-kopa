# Values

What makes people happy is other people. Everything here exists to serve that.

## Connection

People need people. Loneliness is common and quiet, and it is not a personal failure. A friend, a group, a shared evening can change a life.

## Belonging

Having a place where you are expected, where someone notices when you are missing.

## Being yourself

Room to show up as you are, to find out who you are, and to feel free around others.

## Courage to reach out

The first step toward strangers is the hardest one. We make it smaller, on both sides: for the person looking for a group and for the group looking for people.

## Presence

Life happens face to face. A screen is only the way there.

## Kindness and trust

Strangers become friends only when it feels safe. We protect that feeling.

## Passing it on

Someone who found their spark helps the next person find theirs.
