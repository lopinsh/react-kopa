# Why it exists

Someone was lonely. Through Ejam Kopā they found new friends and a community, maybe even a person to share their life with. They discovered a new passion and got their spark back. Now they help others find theirs.

That is the whole purpose. Most platforms for meeting people are built to keep people on the screen, because attention is what they sell. Ejam Kopā is non-profit and wants the opposite: to help people find each other, and then step away and meet.

## Values

What makes people happy is other people. Everything here exists to serve that.

- **Connection.** People need people. Loneliness is common and quiet, and it is not a personal failure.
- **Belonging.** A place where you are expected, where someone notices when you are missing.
- **Being yourself.** Room to show up as you are, to find out who you are, and to feel free around others.
- **Courage to reach out.** The first step toward strangers is the hardest. We make it smaller on both sides: for the person looking for a group and for the group looking for people.
- **Presence.** Life happens face to face. A screen is only the way there.
- **Kindness and trust.** Strangers become friends only when it feels safe. We protect that feeling.
- **Passing it on.** Someone who found their spark helps the next person find theirs.

## The core loop

**Create a group → be found → join → talk → meet.** Groups are the heart; events are what groups do together; hubs (kopienas) gather the groups of one topic or city.

Every feature, fix and idea is judged against this loop. If it doesn't make the loop work better, it waits.

## Typical situations

- **A gym buddy in Jelgava.** None of your friends live in your city, and a classifieds ad feels weird. Instead: a group "gym, beginner level, Jelgava" that interested people join.
- **A book club, a hiking crew, a board-game night.** Small informal groups that today only exist if you already know someone.
- **Official groups** (choirs, dance groups) that today advertise with posters in culture centres.
- **Events from groups.** Public ones anyone can join (a concert, a cleanup) and members-only ones (a rehearsal).

The real job is removing the social awkwardness of reaching out, on both sides.

## Scope and how we work

Hyper-local in Latvia, Latvian first with full English parity. Built so it could grow to other countries, but not designed for that yet.

Built by one person with a full-time job and a young family: small self-contained steps, each leaving the site better, the next step written down in `docs/execution_handoff.md`.
