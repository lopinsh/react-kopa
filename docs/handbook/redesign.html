Agreed so far

  • Identity first

    No screen is redesigned before the identity is settled. This page stays discussion only.

  • Fonts and shapes

    Literata headings, Commissioner text with small italic notes; soft corners, pill buttons, thin dividing lines, quiet labels.

  • Colour means category

    The 15 category colours appear only as dots, icons, soft tints and pictures, never as buttons. The site's own colour is natural and none of them.

  • Categories with icons, in a drawer

    All 15 always listed, alphabetical, each with its icon. The drawer folds to an icon rail on desktop; on phones a "Kategorijas" sheet.

  • Subcategories matter

    Shown after the category on cards, events and the group header: all of them, smaller and lighter than the category.

  • "Ko meklējam?"

    A new optional group field (up to 60 characters) shown as the card's note; when empty, the card shows the next meeting.

  • Two levels of group navigation

    Tabs for the group's pages; a small switch inside a page (Events: upcoming · past; Members: members · requests; Settings: group · sections · owner).

  • Encourage, don't count

    Faces of who is coming instead of "0 apstiprināti"; sentence case, no capitals.

Still open

Which natural colour?

Sūna, Jūra or Rudzu maize. Switch at the top and comment.

Group tabs replace the group sidebar

The layouts above assume it. Confirm, or say what the sidebar should keep.

Then

Once both are settled, the agreed parts become Handbook rules and real components, screen by screen, starting with discovery.

What I saw on the live site (10 October)
  • The brand has no fixed face. "EK" badge, spaced capitals, a gradient slogan; on a group page the logo takes the group's colour.
  • The site's purple is a category colour. Indigo buttons match the Music category; every page has a different main colour.
  • A wall of 15 coloured icons opens discovery and pushes the groups aside.
  • Cards are mostly photo; the useful facts are small.
  • Capital letters everywhere read as shouting.
  • Two navigations on a group (sidebar and tabs) around very little content.
  • Numbers that discourage: "0 apstiprināti", "1 Pasākumi atrasti".
  • Settings in the spotlight: language and theme switches in every header.
