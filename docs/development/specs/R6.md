## Orchestrator defaults (tell owner)

What the rules and drawings leave open; one default each, to be confirmed by the owner later.

1. **Panels open from every page** (redesigned 14 Oct, section 2): a soft click on a group's or an event's address opens its panel over the page you are on (discovery, a group's Pasākumi, Manas grupas, Ziņas, profiles, notifications), as the redesign draws for event rows. The full page opens instead for a group's home from inside that same group, for an event from its own forms, and from `/create` and `/admin` (table in 2.3). Within a group, its Informācija link loads the page in full (a reload) and its part tabs switch without loading.
2. **"Atvērt grupas lapu", "Visa informācija" and the event panel's "Rīko {group}"** load the full page in place of the panel's history entry (a full page load, like opening a shared link): Back from that page returns to the list, not to the panel.
3. **Signed out.** The panel's button ("Sazināties", "Eju", "Pieteikties", "Pieteikties gaidīšanas sarakstā") opens the sign-in pop-up. After signing in you land on the group's or event's own page (the panel's address) and the step continues there once: the contact form opens (as on a group page today); "Eju" or the request is sent, its result shown in place.
4. **Faces.** Signed in: up to three faces and one name ("Ilze un vēl 11 iet", "Ilze iet"; "Tu un vēl 11 iet" when you go). Only people going (never asking or waiting); someone whose profile is not public is counted but never shown or named. Signed out: "12 iet". Nobody going: no line.
5. **After "Eju"** the button becomes a status chip "✓ Eju" with a quiet "Atteikties" next to it (no question: pressing "Eju" again undoes it). Leaving an approved place or the waiting list asks in a pop-over at the button (OD-06: you may not get it back); withdrawing a request does not ask. No toast: the result shows in place.
6. **Panel text.** The group's home text (only when the viewer may read it) or the event's description, plain text, at most 400 characters; "Svarīga informācija" (instructions) never appears in a panel; an online event says "Tiešsaistē" without its link (the link stays on the event page).
7. **Links inside panels.** The group panel's next meeting opens that event's panel (Back returns to the group panel); the event panel's "Rīko {group}" opens the group's page (Default 2). Members and the team see no "Sazināties": "Atvērt grupas lapu" becomes their primary button. The organiser of an approval event sees no step in the panel (as on the event page); "Visa informācija" leads to "Rīkotājiem".
8. **Picture and size.** The event's own image, else the group's image (AR-59 note), else the category tone with its icon. From 768 px the panel is 400 px wide at the right, full height; below 768 px a sheet up to 90 % of the screen height.
9. **Row words (AR-56).** "Pieteikšanās" for an approval event, "Pilns" when the organiser marked it full; after the city, in the same quiet text. Kept next to "Gaida" after you ask (as `evCase` draws). An ended event in a panel shows "Šis pasākums jau ir noticis." and no button.
10. **Members-only and gone.** An outsider who reaches a members-only event in a panel (a stale list) gets one sentence and "Sazināties"; on the event page the notice becomes the same one sentence, and the banner's "Sazināties" is the action (OD-19). A group or event that was hidden or deleted shows one sentence ("… vairs nav pieejama") in the panel.
11. **The tab title** stays discovery's while a panel is open (no metadata in the panel routes).

## 0. Motivation and design alignment

- **Service Law.** Each panel page makes one service call that returns its whole context: `GroupService.getGroupPanel` (composes the request-cached `getGroupWithContext` and `EventService.getNextVisibleEvent`, then narrows) and `EventService.getEventPanel` (one event query, the viewer's access, the going count, faces only for signed-in viewers). Mappers live next to the services (`lib/services/group-panel.ts`, `lib/services/event-panel.ts`) the way `group-card.ts` and `event-row.ts` do. No Prisma in pages, the proxy or components.
- **D12 privacy in the service, not the UI.** `getEventPanel` does not even query user fields for a signed-out viewer (`people: null`), selects only GOING attendees with a public profile, never selects `instructions`, and returns the members-only gate (group facts only) instead of the event for outsiders. The UI renders what it gets.
- **Context Law.** Panels sit outside any group page, so they take their data as props from the panel page (as `ContactForm` already does, built for this in R3b). The discovery list keeps reading `DiscoveryProvider`; `useDiscoveryNav` stops reading the live URL (section 5.4), so the filter line under an open panel keeps the list's filters.
- **Taxonomy Law / AR-06.** The panel's tone (`--c`: dot, picture fallback, icon) comes from the service's category colour on the panel element, as on the card and the row; every button, link, chip and focus ring is site colour. No `--accent` in panels (they are not inside a group layout).
- **Defensive Coding.** Every step button and the undo run in `useTransition` with `disabled` while pending; the contact form keeps its own guard. The panel pages never call `notFound()` (it would render the app's 404 inside the slot); they render the "gone" sentence.
- **Action Consistency.** No new action and no new error code: `setAttendance`, `requestToJoin`, `cancelAttendance` (event), `joinGroup`, `sendInquiry` (group) are reused; their codes (`EVENT_FULL`, `EVENT_PAST`, `MEMBERS_ONLY`, `EVENT_MODE_MISMATCH`, `ALREADY_REQUESTED`, `UNAUTHORIZED`) map to `errors.*`. `setEventFull` gains `updateTag('events')` (the row's "Pilns" comes from the cached list).
- **Zero-Any / Constants.** Typed unions for the join step; new `FACES_MAX = 3`, `PANEL_TEXT_MAX = 400` in `lib/constants`; UI names added to `UI`.

## 1. Data: none

No schema change and no migration. Faces read `Attendance` + `User` (`isProfilePublic`, `image`, `avatarSeed`); the row's word reads `Event.joinMode` and `Event.isFull`; the panel text reads `GroupSection`/`EventTranslation`. Proof P7 (section 10) must be empty.

## 2. Routes: the panels' addresses

**Redesigned after the step-2 stop point (14 Oct; prototyped, checked and committed as `feat(R6): panel routes`).** The first design (the slot in a `(discovery)` layout, a `Next-Url` guard in the proxy) failed: interception matched from every page, and any change to `next-url` in the proxy makes Next answer 307 with a new `_rsc` hash, which loops the client. The design below is Next's own photo-modal pattern, scoped by one server-side rule.

### 2.1 How it works (verified against `node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/03-file-conventions/{intercepting-routes,parallel-routes,default}.md` and the Next 16.4 source)

- **The slot is in `app/[locale]/layout.tsx`** (`{children}` and `{panel}`). A soft navigation (a `<Link>`, `router.push`) to a group's or an event's address is intercepted from **any page** under `/[locale]`: the page you are on stays rendered under the panel (the client reuses it: `client/components/render-tree.js`, the "default segment" branch), and the address bar shows the group's or event's own address. A reload, a shared link or a new tab renders the full page (no `Next-Url` header, no interception). This matches the redesign (the same panel from discovery, a group's Pasākumi, Manas grupas and search).
- **Why routing alone cannot narrow it:** interception is a `beforeFiles` rewrite conditioned on the router's `Next-Url` header (the current page's route path) matching the intercepting route **or any path below it** (`dist/lib/generate-interception-routes-rewrites.js`; route groups are stripped, so a slot in any route group under `[locale]` intercepts from everywhere too). `beforeFiles` rewrites chain without a filesystem check, so an identity rewrite in `next.config` cannot stop it (prototyped: it does not), and the proxy cannot drop or change `next-url` (the `_rsc` cache-busting hash covers it: 307 loop).
- **The one rule, server side, in the panel pages:** `wantsFullPage(nextUrl, target)` in `lib/panel-routes.ts` reads the `next-url` request header (`headers()`; Next keeps it on interception responses) and returns true when the full page is what the person asked for:
  - a group's home from inside the **same group** (its tabs and pages, its event pages, an open panel of that group or of one of its events, the save in its settings);
  - an event from **its own address** (an open event panel) or **its own forms** (`…/events/{slug}/edit`, `…/create-event`: the save redirect);
  - anything from **`/create`** (the group wizard's redirect) or **`/admin`**.
  The page then renders `FullPageInstead` (client: `window.location.replace(location.href)`; the address is already the page's): a full load in place of the history entry. This is the **safety net**; links that know they want the page skip it (2.4).
- **Closing:** `router.back()` (×, Esc, the dim area) or the browser's Back: Next restores the previous entry's tree, the page under the panel unchanged. A soft navigation to any address that is not a panel closes it: the slot has `page.tsx` (for `/[locale]`) and `[...rest]/page.tsx` (everything else), both `null` (`parallel-routes.md`, "Closing the modal": an unmatched slot keeps showing its last state).
- **`children` default:** `app/[locale]/default.tsx` calls `notFound()`. On an intercepted render the client ignores it and keeps the page under the panel; for an unknown address it keeps the 404 (without it, `@panel/[...rest]` makes every address "exist" and an unknown one renders an empty page with status 200: prototyped).

### 2.4 Links that want the full page (no detour through the safety net)

The safety net works everywhere, but it shows the panel skeleton for a moment and loads twice. Links that know they want the page skip it:
- **In panels:** "Atvērt grupas lapu", "Visa informācija" and the event panel's "Rīko {group}" are `FullPageLink` (exists: a plain `<a>` whose plain left click does `window.location.replace(href)`), so Back from that page returns to the list, not to the panel.
- **The group frame's links to its own home:** the Informācija link in `GroupPagesNav` / `GroupPagesDrawer` and any other link to the group's base address rendered inside `[groupSlug]/…` (grep `href={base}`, the pages list's `href`, `groupHref`) become a plain `<a href>` (a full load; prototyped: a soft `history.pushState` + `router.refresh()` variant breaks Back). Informācija's **part tabs** (`?part=` on the same address) switch on the client instead of navigating: the page renders every part this viewer may read, `GroupPartTabs` shows one and writes `?part=` with `history.replaceState` (still shareable: a load with `?part=` opens that part).
- **Redirects after a save** that land on the same group or on the event (`GroupSettingsForm`, `EventCreationWizard`, the event edit page, `EventManageActions` after delete, the create-group wizard): `window.location.assign(href)` (grep `router.push(` and `redirect(` with a group or event path). The safety net catches any that are missed.

### 3.1 `GroupService.getGroupPanel` + `lib/services/group-panel.ts` (new, ≤ 90 lines)

```ts

    id: string; name: string; slug: string; l1Slug: string;
    category: { l1Title: string; color: string; subCategories: string[] };
    city: string; memberCount: number; image: string | null;
```

## 11. Risks

- **Interception from everywhere** is by design now (2.1); the cases that want the page are decided by `wantsFullPage` on the server (the safety net) and, for known links, by plain or `FullPageLink` anchors (2.4). A new link to a group's or an event's page from a form or from inside its own group must use them, or it shows the panel skeleton and reloads. R14 checks the rule.
- **Next's client cache keys** include `Next-Url`, so a group address is cached per page you came from (panel or page); the checks run back to back for this reason.
- **The page under a panel stays mounted:** its client state (a half-typed message on Ziņas, an open drop-down) survives the panel; Overlay's scroll lock covers `#main-content` on every page.
- **Default-slot reuse after actions.** An action that revalidates refreshes the intercepted route; the list is reused from the client (Next 16.4). R7 checks the row afterwards; 5.5 is the fallback.
- **The live URL under a panel** is the panel's: anything on discovery that reads `useSearchParams()`/`usePathname()` changes while it is open. `useDiscoveryNav` moves to the provider (5.4); the header's search field, the footer and the language menu read the panel's address while it is open (they are under the dim; after closing they return). Note for R7, whose search reads the URL.
- **Full loads.** "Atvērt grupas lapu", "Visa informācija", "Rīko" and a group's own Informācija link are full document loads (Defaults 1–2); acceptable, the same as a shared link.
- **Privacy.** Faces and members-only content are decided in the services; the reviewer's list checks the payloads, not the screen.

## 13. For later specs

Every soft link to a group's or an event's address opens its panel from any page (2.1), so R7's results and R8's rows get panels without routing work; they pass `opensPanel` (no scroll) on their links. A new form or page that redirects to its own group or event uses a full-page link (2.4), and `wantsFullPage` gains a line if a new kind of source should never show panels.
