## Orchestrator defaults (tell owner)

What the rules and drawings leave open; one default each, to be confirmed by the owner later.

1. **Panels open from discovery only** (Grupas and Pasākumi, every card, row and event row there). A group or event link anywhere else (a group's pages, Manas grupas, Ziņas, profiles, notifications) opens the full page, as the card says. R7 (search) and R8 (Manas grupas) decide in their specs whether their lists open panels (section 13).
2. **"Atvērt grupas lapu" and "Visa informācija"** load the full page in place of the panel's history entry (a full page load, like opening a shared link): Back from that page returns to the list, not to the panel.
3. **Signed out.** The panel's button ("Sazināties", "Eju", "Pieteikties", "Pieteikties gaidīšanas sarakstā") opens the sign-in pop-up. After signing in you land on the group's or event's own page (the panel's address) and the step continues there once: the contact form opens (as on a group page today); "Eju" or the request is sent, its result shown in place.
4. **Faces.** Signed in: up to three faces and one name ("Ilze un vēl 11 iet", "Ilze iet"; "Tu un vēl 11 iet" when you go). Only people going (never asking or waiting); someone whose profile is not public is counted but never shown or named. Signed out: "12 iet". Nobody going: no line.
5. **After "Eju"** the button becomes a status chip "✓ Eju" with a quiet "Atteikties" next to it (no question: pressing "Eju" again undoes it). Leaving an approved place or the waiting list asks in a pop-over at the button (OD-06: you may not get it back); withdrawing a request does not ask. No toast: the result shows in place.
6. **Panel text.** The group's home text (only when the viewer may read it) or the event's description, plain text, at most 400 characters; "Svarīga informācija" (instructions) never appears in a panel; an online event says "Tiešsaistē" without its link (the link stays on the event page).
7. **Links inside panels.** The group panel's next meeting opens that event's panel; the event panel's "Rīko {group}" opens the group's panel; Back walks back through them. Members and the team see no "Sazināties": "Atvērt grupas lapu" becomes their primary button. The organiser of an approval event sees no step in the panel (as on the event page); "Visa informācija" leads to "Rīkotājiem".
8. **Picture and size.** The event's own image, else the group's image (AR-59 note), else the category tone with its icon. From 768 px the panel is 400 px wide at the right, full height; below 768 px a sheet up to 90 % of the screen height.
9. **Row words (AR-56).** "Pieteikšanās" for an approval event, "Pilns" when the organiser marked it full; after the city, in the same quiet text. Kept next to "Gaida" after you ask (as `evCase` draws). An ended event in a panel shows "Šis pasākums jau ir noticis." and no button.
10. **Members-only and gone.** An outsider who reaches a members-only event in a panel (a stale list) gets one sentence and "Sazināties"; on the event page the notice becomes the same one sentence, and the banner's "Sazināties" is the action (OD-19). A group or event that was hidden or deleted shows one sentence ("… vairs nav pieejama") in the panel.
11. **The tab title** stays discovery's while a panel is open (no metadata in the panel routes).

## 0. Motivation and design alignment

- **Service Law.** Each panel page makes one service call that returns its whole context: `GroupService.getGroupPanel` (composes the request-cached `getGroupWithContext` and `EventService.getNextVisibleEvent`, then narrows) and `EventService.getEventPanel` (one event query, the viewer's access, the going count, faces only for signed-in viewers). Mappers live next to the services (`lib/services/group-panel.ts`, `lib/services/event-panel.ts`) the way `group-card.ts` and `event-row.ts` do. No Prisma in pages, the proxy or components.
- **D12 privacy in the service, not the UI.** `getEventPanel` does not even query user fields for a signed-out viewer (`people: null`), selects only GOING attendees with a public profile, never selects `instructions`, and returns the members-only gate (group facts only) instead of the event for outsiders. The UI renders what it gets.
- **Context Law.** Panels sit outside any group page, so they take their data as props from the panel page (as `ContactForm` already does, built for this in R3b). The discovery list keeps reading `DiscoveryProvider`; `useDiscoveryNav` stops reading the live URL (section 5.4), so the filter line under an open panel keeps the list's filters.
- **Taxonomy Law / AR-06.** The panel's tone (`--c`: dot, picture fallback, icon) comes from the service's category colour on the panel element, as on the card and the row; every button, link, chip and focus ring is site colour. No `--accent` in panels (they are not inside a group layout).
- **Defensive Coding.** Every step button and the undo run in `useTransition` with `disabled` while pending; the contact form keeps its own guard. The panel pages never call `notFound()` (it would render the app's 404 inside the slot); they render the "gone" sentence.
- **Action Consistency.** No new action and no new error code: `setAttendance`, `requestToJoin`, `cancelAttendance` (event), `joinGroup`, `sendInquiry` (group) are reused; their codes (`EVENT_FULL`, `EVENT_PAST`, `MEMBERS_ONLY`, `EVENT_MODE_MISMATCH`, `ALREADY_REQUESTED`, `UNAUTHORIZED`) map to `errors.*`. `setEventFull` gains `updateTag('events')` (the row's "Pilns" comes from the cached list).
- **Zero-Any / Constants.** Typed unions for the join step; new `FACES_MAX = 3`, `PANEL_TEXT_MAX = 400` in `lib/constants`; UI names added to `UI`.

## 2. Routes: the panels' addresses

### 2.1 Why these files (verified against `node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/03-file-conventions/{intercepting-routes,parallel-routes,default,route-groups}.md` and the Next 16.4 source)

- An intercepting route renders another route's page "within the current layout" on a **soft navigation** (a `<Link>` or `router.push`); a reload or a shared link renders the real page. `(.)` matches on **route segments**, ignoring `@slot` folders and route groups (`intercepting-routes.md`, "Good to know"), so `(.)[l1Slug]` inside `app/[locale]/(discovery)/@panel/` intercepts `/[locale]/[l1Slug]/…`.
- The slot `@panel` is a prop of the layout in its folder. A `default.tsx` is needed for the slot and for `children` whenever Next cannot recover a slot's state (`default.md`): an intercepted render sends `children` as the default segment, and the client keeps the page already shown there (Next 16.4 `client/components/render-tree.js`, the "default segment … the client reuses whatever segment was already active" branch).
- **The catch the docs don't state:** interception is a rewrite that applies when the router's `Next-Url` header (the current page's route path) matches the intercepting route **or any path below it** (`dist/lib/generate-interception-routes-rewrites.js`: the trailing `(?:/)?` becomes `(?:/.*)?`; route groups are stripped by `normalizeAppPath`). Our intercepting route is `/[locale]`, so without a guard a group link on `/lv/messages` or an event row on a group's Pasākumi would be intercepted too, and render the discovery layout with an empty `children`. The guard is in the proxy (2.3): the `Next-Url` header is dropped unless the click came from discovery, so the rewrite does not apply and the full page renders. `proxy.ts` runs before the `beforeFiles` rewrites where interception lives (`router-utils/resolve-routes.js` applies the proxy's request-header override first), and next-intl's `next()` forwards the request headers it was given (`next-intl/dist/esm/*/middleware/middleware.js`), so a copy of the request without the header is enough.
- **Why a `(discovery)` layout, not `app/[locale]/layout.tsx`:** a slot in the locale layout would put the panel tree (and its `default.tsx`) into every page of the site; the card wants panels over discovery only, and the discovery route group (R2b) already holds exactly the discovery page.
- `router.back()` closes the panel (`parallel-routes.md`, "Closing the modal"): the browser's previous entry is the list, which Next restores from its history state with the list as it was.
- A soft navigation to discovery itself (logo, a filter) must close an open panel: unmatched slots keep their state on soft navigation (`parallel-routes.md`, "Good to know" under Modals), so `@panel/page.tsx` returns `null` for `/[locale]`.

### 2.3 `lib/panel-routes.ts` and the proxy guard

```ts
import { LOCALES } from '@/lib/constants';
const L = LOCALES.join('|');
/** Discovery's route path as the router sends it in Next-Url (no search, route groups stripped): '/lv', '/en'. */
export const isPanelSource = (nextUrl: string): boolean => new RegExp(`^/(${L})/?$`).test(nextUrl);
/** A page a panel can stand in for: a group's home or one event. */
export const isPanelTarget = (pathname: string): boolean => new RegExp(`^/(${L})/[^/]+/group/[^/]+(/events/[^/]+)?/?$`).test(pathname);
```
`proxy.ts`, before `return intlMiddleware(req)`:
```ts
// Panels (R6) open only over discovery: interception applies whenever Next-Url is under /[locale], so drop it for clicks from anywhere else.
function withoutForeignNextUrl(req: NextRequest): NextRequest {
    const from = req.headers.get('next-url');
    if (!from || isPanelSource(from) || !isPanelTarget(req.nextUrl.pathname)) return req;
    if (req.method !== 'GET' && req.method !== 'HEAD') return req;
    const headers = new Headers(req.headers);
    headers.delete('next-url');
    return new NextRequest(req.url, { headers, method: req.method });
}
return intlMiddleware(withoutForeignNextUrl(req));
```
Do not mutate `req.headers` in place. Server actions sent from an open panel carry `Next-Url: /lv` (Next sends the referring Next-Url while an interception route is in the tree), so they keep the panel.

```ts
    group: { name: string; slug: string; city: string };
    join: EventJoinView;
    going: { count: number; viewerGoing: boolean; people: Face[] | null };   // people null when signed out (D12)
}
export type EventPanelResult =
    | { kind: 'event'; event: EventPanelData }
    | { kind: 'membersOnly'; group: { id: string; name: string; isAcceptingMembers: boolean; canContact: boolean } }
    | null;
static getEventPanel = cache(async (eventSlug, groupSlug, l1Slug, locale, viewerId?): Promise<EventPanelResult> => …)
```
- One query: the group by `findGroupByPath(groupSlug, l1Slug)` with `hiddenAt: null`, then the event by slug with `EVENT_ROW_INCLUDE(lang)` (titles and descriptions only), the group's `bannerImage`, `city`, `isAcceptingMembers`, and `_count: { select: { attendees: { where: { status: 'GOING' } } } }`. Never `include: { translations: true }` (that would read instructions).
- Access through the private `getAccess(groupId, viewerId)`. A `MEMBERS_ONLY` event for someone who is neither a member nor a site admin → `{ kind: 'membersOnly', group }` with group facts only (`canContact` = signed-in or not, not a member, not pending; the button gates sign-in itself). Missing → `null`.
- `myStatus`: one `attendance.findUnique` when signed in. `canManage = event.creatorId === viewerId || access.isGroupAdmin`. `ended = isEventPast(event)`.
- **Faces (D12):** only when `viewerId` is set: `attendance.findMany({ where: { eventId, status: 'GOING', user: { isProfilePublic: true } }, orderBy: { joinedAt: 'asc' }, take: FACES_MAX, select: { user: { select: { id, name, username, image, avatarSeed } } } })`; the viewer's own row first when they go (their own face even with a private profile: it is them). Signed out: no user query at all, `people: null`.
- Map with `toEventPanel(...)` in `event-panel.ts`: `resolveEventText(e.translations, lang, e.originalLang, true)`, `plainExcerpt(text.description, PANEL_TEXT_MAX)`, category and sub-categories exactly as `toEventRow` (share its helper).

### 3.4 `lib/event-join.ts` (new, pure, ≤ 60 lines): the step

```ts
export type JoinStep =
    | { kind: 'act'; action: 'go' | 'request' | 'waitlist'; note: 'requestNote' | 'fullNote' | null }
    | { kind: 'done'; label: 'going' | 'approved' | 'pending' | 'waitlisted'; undo: 'cancelGoing' | 'leaveEvent' | 'withdrawRequest' | 'leaveWaitlist'; confirm: boolean; note: 'waitlistNote' | null }
    | { kind: 'quiet'; note: 'declined' | 'tookPlace' }
    | { kind: 'none' };                                  // organiser of an approval event
export function joinStepOf(j: EventJoinView): JoinStep
```
The same table as today's `EventParticipation`: ended → quiet `tookPlace`; REQUEST and `canManage` → none; OPEN: GOING → done `going` (undo `cancelGoing`, no confirm), else act `go`; REQUEST: GOING → done `approved` (undo `leaveEvent`, confirm), PENDING → done `pending` (undo `withdrawRequest`, no confirm), DECLINED → quiet `declined`, WAITLISTED and full → done `waitlisted` (undo `leaveWaitlist`, confirm, note `waitlistNote`), full → act `waitlist` (note `fullNote`), else act `request` (note `requestNote`). `rowWordOf(joinState)` → `'request' | 'full' | null` for the row.

## 11. Risks

- **Interception from everywhere.** The `Next-Url` guard in the proxy is what keeps panels on discovery; R5/R10 prove it. If Next changes how the header is matched, the proofs catch it.
- **Next's client cache keys** include `Next-Url`, so a group URL is cached once as a panel (from discovery) and once as a page (from elsewhere); proof R5 runs right after R1 for this reason.
- **Default-slot reuse after actions.** An action that revalidates refreshes the intercepted route; the list is reused from the client (Next 16.4). R7 checks the row afterwards; 5.5 is the fallback.
- **The live URL under a panel** is the panel's: anything on discovery that reads `useSearchParams()`/`usePathname()` changes while it is open. `useDiscoveryNav` moves to the provider (5.4); the header's search field, the footer and the language menu read the panel's address while it is open (they are under the dim; after closing they return). Note for R7, whose search reads the URL.
- **Full page from a panel** is a full document load (Default 2); acceptable, same as a shared link.
- **Privacy.** Faces and members-only content are decided in the services; the reviewer's list checks the payloads, not the screen.

## 12. Out of scope

Panels from a group's Pasākumi, Manas grupas (R8) and search (R7); case 4 "Slēgts" (OD-18); the event page's layout (R9a; only the join box, the members-only word and the notice change here); faces on the event page (R9a: "the panel's content in full"); the tab title while a panel is open; swipe-down to close a sheet.
