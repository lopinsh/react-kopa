# R21 Phone pass (release A)

From the owner's phone notes of 22 Oct (about 384 px). Rule added to `docs/handbook/design.md` (Pop-up): below 768 px every pop-up and drop-down is a bottom sheet; the big panels are full-height sheets; a chat is a full screen.

## Items and root causes

| # | Note | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Footer gap at the bottom | Scroll container reserved 5rem under the footer, the bottom bar is 4rem | 4rem (`app/[locale]/layout.tsx`) |
| 2 | Header search open shape odd | The field squared its bottom corners (to join a drop-down panel) also on the phone screen, where nothing hangs under it | `SearchInput` prop `joined`, off in `SearchOverlay` |
| 3, 4 | Group and event slide-over | Below md the panel was a content-height sheet with the picture's × floating on it | `panel` layout is `h-dvh` below md; `PanelPicture` renders a sticky close bar first; the handle is gone for `panel` |
| 5, 6 | Tēma and date chips cannot select | The sheet's own `history.back()` on unmount swallowed the `router.push` of the choice (Tēma was also a side drawer) | `afterOverlayClose` in `useBackClose`, used by `useDiscoveryNav.go` and `CategoryRow`; Tēma is a bottom sheet, `ListDrawer` removed |
| 7 | Ziņas one column | Two-pane switch was at `sm` (640) and the chat sat inside the card under the own-area bars | switch at `md`; the open chat is `fixed inset-0` below md with its back arrow |
