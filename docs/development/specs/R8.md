## Owner decisions (each with the default the build applies)

No owner question is open. Small choices, each **Default (spec)**:

1. **What counts as "new" (AR-14).** Since `User.myGroupsSeenAt` (or the last 7 days when it is null): upcoming events created in your groups (member or team, not waiting) by someone else; announcements posted there by someone else (not archived); answers to your requests (accepted or declined). The sum shows as a number at the end of "Manas grupas" in the profile menu and as the badge on Manas grupas in the own menu; the header picture gets the small dot (`ICON_DOT`) while it is above 0. Nothing on the bottom bar.
2. **When "seen" is set.** Once Manas grupas has rendered in the browser (a client effect calls `markMyGroupsSeen()`), never during the server render (prefetches and reloads must not count as a visit). The page keeps what it rendered; items newer than the previous visit carry a small site-colour dot ("Jauns" for screen readers).
3. **cmv9amjft applies everywhere.** Any group you belong to (any role) or wait for opens its full page from every list: one rule in the group panel route (3.4) catches every link; lists that already know (Manas grupas, discovery's Grupas) link straight to the full page so there is no panel flash. Events keep their panel (the note is about groups).
4. **Tuvākie pasākumi.** The next 30 days: upcoming events of your groups (members-only included, as on the group's Pasākumi) and events of other groups you are going to, asked for or waiting for; at most 20, under day headings, with the group line and the city. Two columns when wide (as discovery).
5. **Jaunumi.** Announcements of your groups from the last 30 days, at most 10, plus the answers to your requests newer than your last visit (shown once, section 3.2), newest first. The group's name is the only link (to its Jaunumi page for an announcement, its home for an answer).
6. **Empty sections are left out**, and their entry in the section menu with them. With no group and no request at all: the empty state (outline icon, "Pagaidām kluss", one sentence, "Atklāt grupas"). "Tas ir viss jaunais." ends a page that has anything.
7. **The section menu** is the existing part-tab look (`GroupPartTabs` with `onSelect`), sticky under the header, marking the section in view; a tap scrolls to it (`#upcoming`, `#news`, `#groups`, `#requests`, `scroll-margin-top` for the sticky bars).
8. **Dropdown size.** 5 rows, newest first; the panel 360 px wide (`max-w-[calc(100vw-24px)]`), under its icon, right-aligned; opens and closes on click, Enter or Space; closes on Esc (focus back to the icon), a click outside, a row or footer link, and any page change. Fetched when opened (and the bell's counts on load), so a closed dropdown costs nothing.
9. **Phones (< 768 px).** The bell is a link to `/notifications` with its count; the messages icon stays hidden (the bottom bar has Ziņas, as today). Paziņojumi marks "Es" in the bottom bar.
10. **Paziņojumi page paging.** 30 rows a page; "Rādīt vēl" is a link to `?page=n+1` that renders the first 30·n (at most 10 pages), `scroll={false}`; Back works; no endless scrolling (as OD-13). Admins get the two tabs on the page as well (`?tab=admin`).
11. **A declined request now notifies the person** (`APPLICATION_DECLINED`, "Tavs pieteikums šoreiz netika apstiprināts", link to the group). Today declining only deletes the membership and says nothing, so "the answer shown once" would have no source. Behaviour rule "would miss something" holds.
12. **Bell rows.** Type icon (lucide, one map in `lib/notification-icons.ts`); line 1 the headline (`notifications.headline.<TYPE>`, unchanged); line 2 the group as a link and its category line (dot, title, sub-category) or, when there is a quote, the quote; time and the unread dot at the right; "Šodien" / "Agrāk" headings. The whole row opens the notification's link (stretched link; the group link sits above it, like `CategoryLine aboveCardLink`).
13. **Shared groups on personal Ziņas rows.** Up to 2 group names you both belong to (members or team, groups not hidden), then "+N".

## 0. Motivation and design alignment

- **Service Law.** Manas grupas is one call, `UserService.getMyGroupsOverview(userId, locale)`, in a new helper file `lib/services/my-groups.ts` (like `discovery-search.ts`); it returns a typed `MyGroupsOverview`, never raw Prisma rows. Counts come from `UserService.getOwnCounts`; notification lists from `NotificationService.list`. No Prisma outside services; the panel rule asks `GroupService.getViewerMembershipRole`.
- **Context Law.** The own-area layout already provides `MeProvider`; the counts live in a new `OwnCountsProvider` (beside `UnreadMessagesProvider`, in `Providers`), read by `UserMenu`, `MeMenu`, the bell and Manas grupas through `useOwnCounts()`. No count is prop-drilled from the header.
- **Taxonomy Law / AR-06.** Category tones only on dots, discs and soft tints via `--c` from the service; links, the unread dot, tabs and the section menu in the site colour; no `--accent` outside group pages.
- **Defensive Coding.** `markMyGroupsSeen`, `markManyAsRead`, `markAllAsRead`, "Atsaukt pieteikumu" each behind `useTransition` (`isPending` guard); the paging param and tab are parsed with a shared Zod schema (`lib/validations/notification.ts`).
- **Action Consistency.** Every action returns `ActionResponse<T>` with registry codes (`UNAUTHORIZED`, `VALIDATION_FAILED`, `ACTION_FAILED`, `UPDATE_FAILED`, `CANCEL_FAILED`); auth and `revalidatePath` in actions, logic in services.
- **Zero-Any, Constants.** Typed shapes in `lib/types/own-area.ts`; new constants `MY_UPCOMING_DAYS = 30`, `MY_UPCOMING_MAX = 20`, `MY_NEWS_DAYS = 30`, `MY_NEWS_MAX = 10`, `MY_NEW_FALLBACK_DAYS = 7`, `DROPDOWN_ROWS = 5`, `NOTIFICATIONS_PAGE = 30`, `NOTIFICATIONS_MAX_PAGES = 10`, `SHARED_GROUPS_SHOWN = 2` in `lib/constants`, and the `UI` names of section 8.

## 1. Data

**Migration `add_user_my_groups_seen_at`** (expand only; `npm run db:migrate -- --name add_user_my_groups_seen_at`, then `npm run db:generate`): `User.myGroupsSeenAt DateTime?` with the doc comment "When Manas grupas was last opened; its 'new' count counts from here (AR-14)." SQL is one `ALTER TABLE "User" ADD COLUMN "myGroupsSeenAt" TIMESTAMP(3);`. The running app never reads it, so it is safe during the blue/green deploy. No index (read by primary key).

**D13: no `add_notification_group_relation`.** Read on the local DB (72 notifications, 15 Oct): every group-scoped type stores the group in its link, `/<l1>/group/<slug>…` (`APPLICATION_ACCEPTED`, `OWNERSHIP_TRANSFERRED`, `NEW_EVENT`, `EVENT_*`, `JOIN_REQUEST …/members?tab=requests`, `NEW_POST …/announcements`, `CITY_*`), messages types store `/messages?c=<id>` (the group comes from the conversation's `originGroup`, as today), admin types `/admin/…`; only `GROUP_HIDDEN` (and pre-R3 `NEW_POST` rows) have no link and carry `groupName` in their args. `(categoryId, slug)` is unique; the service resolves by slug within the L1 already (`getUserNotifications`). A group that changes category or is deleted loses the link on old rows and falls back to `args.groupName` as plain text: acceptable, no foreign key needed. The new `APPLICATION_DECLINED` stores the group link like `APPLICATION_ACCEPTED`.

### 3.2 `lib/services/my-groups.ts` (new, ≤ 150 lines) and `UserService.getMyGroupsOverview`

`UserService.getMyGroupsOverview(userId, locale)` delegates (3 lines); `getMyGroups` is deleted (its only caller is the old page). Two waves of queries, **at most 10 in total** (stop point check):

1. Wave 1 (parallel): the user's `myGroupsSeenAt`; `membership.findMany({ where: { userId, group: { hiddenAt: null } }, select: { role, joinedAt, group: { select: GROUP_CARD_SELECT(lang) + id } }, orderBy: { joinedAt: 'desc' } })`. `since = seenAt ?? now − MY_NEW_FALLBACK_DAYS`.
2. Split: `memberIds` (OWNER/ADMIN/MEMBER), `teamIds` (OWNER/ADMIN), `pending` (PENDING → `requests`, `since = joinedAt`).
3. Wave 2 (parallel): `membership.groupBy` waiting counts for `teamIds` (as today); upcoming events: `event.findMany({ where: { AND: [{ group: { hiddenAt: null } }, upcoming(today … today+30), { OR: [{ groupId: { in: memberIds } }, { visibility: 'PUBLIC', group: { type: 'PUBLIC' }, attendances: { some: { userId, status: { in: ['GOING','PENDING','WAITLISTED'] } } } }] }] }, include: EVENT_ROW_INCLUDE(lang), orderBy: startDate, take: MY_UPCOMING_MAX })` then one `attendance.findMany` for the tags (reuse `EventService.myTags`, made non-private, or the same query); posts: `post.findMany({ where: { groupId: { in: memberIds }, archivedAt: null, createdAt: { gte: now − 30 d } }, orderBy: { createdAt: 'desc' }, take: MY_NEWS_MAX, select: { id, title, content, createdAt, authorId, group: card-ish select } })` → `isNew = createdAt > since && authorId !== userId`, `excerpt = plainExcerpt(body, 160)` (`legacyTitle` for untitled posts); answers: `notification.findMany({ where: { userId, audience: 'USER', type: { in: ['APPLICATION_ACCEPTED', 'REQUEST_APPROVED', 'APPLICATION_DECLINED'] }, createdAt: { gt: seenAt ?? since } }, orderBy: { createdAt: 'desc' }, take: 10 })`, groups resolved through `NotificationService.resolveGroups` (3.3), name from `args.groupName` when unresolved; `EventService.getNextVisibleEvents(memberIds, locale, userId)` for the cards' note.
4. `upcoming` grouped by Riga day with the helpers discovery uses (`dayInZone`, `startOfTodayInRiga`); `news` = posts + answers sorted by `at` desc.

**"Shown once" (Default 2).** An answer is listed while its notification is newer than the visit before this one. The page reads `seenAt` during render and never writes; `ownArea/MarkMyGroupsSeen.tsx` calls the action after mount. A reload or a later visit no longer lists it; Back/forward restores the rendered page from the router's history cache (unchanged); the bell keeps the notification. Never filter answers by `read`.

### 3.3 `NotificationService` (edit, file ≤ 220 lines after the change)

- `list(userId, { audience, take, skip?, locale })` → `{ rows: NotificationView[]; hasMore: boolean }`: admin rows only for a current site admin (else `rows: []`); fetches `take + 1` (newest first), folds unread with `groupUnread` (kept), resolves groups. Replaces `getUserNotifications`.
- `resolveGroups(links, locale)` (extracted from today's body, exported for 3.2): link → `NewsGroup` (`name`, `path` = `/<l1>/group/<slug>`, `l1Slug`, `color` = the L1 colour the cards use, `categoryTitle`, `subCategory`), two queries at most (groups by slug, conversations by id).
- `getUnreadCounts(userId)` → `{ user, admin }` from one `notification.groupBy({ by: ['audience'], where: { userId, read: false }, _count: true })` (admin 0 unless site admin).
- `NotificationPayload['type']` gains `'APPLICATION_DECLINED'`.

`UserService.getOwnCounts(userId)` (in `my-groups.ts`, delegated) → `OwnCounts`: `getUnreadCounts` + three `count` queries (new events, new posts, answers since `since`, the definitions of Default 1), all in parallel. `UserService.markMyGroupsSeen(userId)` sets `myGroupsSeenAt = now()`.

## 12. Risks

- **Query count on Manas grupas.** Everything in two parallel waves; no per-group loop (no `getNextVisibleEvent` per card, no count per group). Q1 is the guard. Nothing here is cached per user (`unstable_cache` would mix viewers).
- **"Answer shown once".** Never write during render (prefetches, reloads, streaming retries); the client effect runs once per mount (`useRef` guard in dev's double effects). If the action fails the answer simply shows again next time (safe direction). The bell keeps the record.
- **Full page for your groups.** The safety net reloads the page after a panel shell starts (a brief skeleton) for lists that do not know your groups (search, profiles, event rows); the known lists avoid it. `FullPageInstead` replaces the history entry, so Back still works.
- **Counts drifting.** Counts refresh on load, focus, realtime and after each mark; they are hints, the lists are the truth.
