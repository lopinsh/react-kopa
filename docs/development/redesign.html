Today's category colours are bright web colours (pink, lime, violet), louder than the natural site colour around them. The proposal is fifteen separate tones from Latvian nature. Each is a darker, calmer cousin of today's hue, so a category keeps roughly the colour people already know. Filters, for hundreds of groups and events

You asked for filters to be thought through. Below: what comparable sites do, the filter set I propose and where each filter lives, where sub-categories go, how to pick the place for someone we don't know, and events held away from the group's home city.

What others do with filters (short)

Meetup

Starts from your location, with a distance in km. Above the results is one line of small dropdowns: any day, any type (online or in person), distance, category. They look like plain words until opened, and a chosen one turns filled.

Eventbrite

The city is in the address and the page title. Quick date chips (Today, Tomorrow, This weekend) and "Free" sit above the list. More options are in a side column on desktop and a "Filters" sheet on phones. The list is grouped by date.

Airbnb

Place, dates and guests are in the search pill. Everything else is behind one "Filters" button: a window with counts ("Show 312 places") that update as you choose. A sideways row of category icons sits above the results.

Facebook Events

Location, date and category in the left column. No filter bar over the content.

The common thread

Place is always the first filter, and always visible. Date matters only for events. A few filters stay in sight; the rest go behind one button, but only when there are many. Counts show what a choice will give before you make it.

What we take

Five filters, all visible, and no "Filtri" window, because we don't have enough filters to need one. Counts on every choice. A chosen filter shows as a chip you can remove.

The filter set

  • VietaalwaysNext to the switch. "Visa Latvija" or a city, with your profile city first.
  • Grupas / PasākumialwaysThe switch. It is the page's main choice.
  • KategorijaalwaysThe drawer on desktop, all fifteen with counts. On phones, a button that opens a sheet.
  • Apakškategorijaafter a categoryChips above the results. Several can be on at once.

The place, for someone we don't know

Signed in: the profile city, plus "Mani interesē arī", a few other cities you choose in your profile. Signed out, we know nothing, and we don't guess from the internet address. The answer is kept on the device, and the question doesn't come back.

Why admin had no bottom bar

Admin pages use their own layout today. It was built before the bottom bar and simply leaves it out, so it was never a decision. Recommendation: admin is a place inside the site, so it keeps the site's header and bottom bar like any other page, and only adds its own menu, in the drawer. An admin can then jump to Ziņas or Atklāt from anywhere without a "back to the site" link.

A top menu: gains and drawbacks

Gains: every section is visible at a glance, which is the usual pattern for a handful of sections (GitHub pairs tabs across the top with a settings list down the left in exactly this way), and tables keep the full width. Drawbacks: it fits about eight items, so a ninth would need "Vairāk". Below about 900 px it moves into the drawer.

The rule · for the Handbook (Behaviour patterns)
  1. Two levels at most: an area's pages (a group's pages, admin's sections) and a page's parts (Par mums…, Gaidāmie · Pagājušie, Handbook chapters, Dizains parts).
  2. One level runs across, the other down. Never two rows across, stacked, and never two lists down, side by side.
  3. Groups: pages down the left, parts across as tabs. Admin: sections across the top, parts down the left, because admin has many parts (ten Handbook chapters).
  4. Phones: the pages level goes into a drawer from the left, opened by ☰ next to the place's name. A group's few parts stay tabs; admin's many parts sit in the same drawer, under their section.
  5. The header is the same everywhere, admin included: logo, search, +, messages, notifications, the admin icon (admins only) and you. No section menus in it. The bottom bar is the same everywhere on phones.
