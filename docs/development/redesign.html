The bottom bar on admin pages Agreed

Admin is a place inside the site, so it keeps the site's header and bottom bar like any other page, and only adds its own menu, in the drawer. An admin can then jump to Ziņas or Atklāt from anywhere without a "back to the site" link.

A top menu: gains and drawbacks

Gains: every section is visible at a glance, which is the usual pattern for a handful of sections (GitHub pairs tabs across the top with a settings list down the left in exactly this way), and tables keep the full width. With five sections there is room to grow. Drawbacks: about eight fit, so a ninth would need "Vairāk". Below about 900 px it moves into the drawer.

The rule · for the Handbook (Behaviour patterns)
  1. Two levels of menu: an area's pages (a group's pages, admin's sections) and a page's parts (Par mums…, Gaidāmie · Pagājušie, admin's pages). In admin, a page with parts of its own (Dizains, Rokasgrāmata) opens them indented under it in the same list, never as a third menu.
  2. One level runs across, the other down. Never two rows across, stacked, and never two lists down, side by side.
  3. Groups: pages down the left, parts across as tabs. Admin: five sections across the top, their pages down the left, because admin has many (ten Handbook chapters).
  4. Phones: the pages level goes into a drawer from the left, opened by ☰ next to the place's name. A group's few parts stay tabs; admin's many pages sit in the same drawer, under their section.
  5. The header is the same everywhere, admin included: logo, search, +, the admin shield (admins only), messages, notifications, and your picture. No light/dark switch and no section menus in it. The bottom bar is the same everywhere on phones.
