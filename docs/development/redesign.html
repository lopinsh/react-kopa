Hundreds of groups and events

Your worry: one day there may be hundreds of groups with hundreds of events, and the page must make sense before anything is filtered. Below: what comparable sites do, the narrowing you suggested (place → category → sub-category, with counts), and the events view on desktop.

What others do (short)

Meetup

Results start from your location. One line of small dropdown filters sits above the results (day, type, distance, category); they look like plain words until opened, so they don't compete with the events.

Eventbrite

The city is part of the address and of the page title ("events in …"); you change it from the title. Category and date are quick choices; the list itself is grouped by date.

Airbnb

Place comes first, in the search pill. Everything else hides behind a single "Filters" button that opens a window, and a row of category icons scrolls sideways above the results.

Facebook Events

Filters live in a column on the left (location, date, category); the content column carries no filter bar at all.

Latvian city portals

Sites like Visit Daugavpils are already one city: a list of categories, each opening a date-ordered list. No search across places.

What we take

Place first and always visible; filters at the side, not on top; counts so people see where things are; date grouping for events; switching views is a small control at the edge of the title, never a row of its own.

Narrowing with counts

Your idea, recommended as is: every step shows how many groups (or events, on the Pasākumi view) are behind each choice, and empty choices stay in the list, greyed, so the 15 categories never jump around.

Search and place

Three of your questions: should search be limited to a city, what "Rādīt visus rezultātus" opens, and the city list with its search field inside.

Search starts in your place

How others do it: Airbnb asks for the place before anything else; Meetup searches around your location and lets you change it on the results; Eventbrite keeps a second "where" field next to the search words. Recommendation: no second field. The search uses the page's place (your city), and the first line of the open panel says so, with "Visā Latvijā" one tap away. Results from other cities are not mixed in; a quiet line counts them.

The rule · for the Handbook (Behaviour patterns)
  1. Desktop: a page's sections are a menu on the left, with each section's own parts nested under it. It scrolls with the page and is never sticky.
  2. Phones: up to five sections are tabs under the title (group pages); more than five open from one selector at the top of the page (admin, Handbook).
  3. The global header never carries section menus. It holds the site, Atklāt and Manas grupas, search, messages, notifications, the admin icon (admins only) and the person.
  4. Never two menus stacked. Views of the same thing (Gaidāmie · Pagājušie) are nested in the left menu on desktop, a small switch on phones.
  • Admin7 sectionsLeft menu on desktop, selector on phones.
  • Handbook10 chaptersSub-items of "Rokasgrāmata" in the admin menu.
  • Group page4 pagesSidebar on desktop (shown above), tabs on phones.
  • Messagesa listNot a menu: a list and a conversation, and the side drawer already agreed.

Agreed so far

  • Identity first

    No screen is redesigned before the identity is settled. This page stays discussion only.

  • Fonts and shapes

    Literata headings, Commissioner text with small italic notes; soft corners, pill buttons, thin dividing lines, quiet labels.

  • Colour means category

    Category colours appear only as dots, icons, soft tints, banners and the picture fallback, never as site buttons. The site's own colour is natural and none of them.

  • Categories with icons, in a drawer

    All 15 always listed, alphabetical, each with its icon. The drawer folds to an icon rail on desktop; on phones a "Kategorijas" sheet.

  • Sub-categories matter

    Shown after the category on cards, events, the group header and group conversations: all of them, smaller and lighter.

  • Group cards

    Horizontal on desktop, vertical on phones (2026-10-11). "Ko meklējam?" note, or the next meeting.

  • Group header

    The coloured header with the group's mark, title and category is the frame of every group page; the cover-photo version is dropped.

  • Group page order

    Informācija, Jaunumi, Pasākumi, Biedri (2026-10-11).

  • An opened card sits on top

    Panel from the right on desktop, sheet on phones (owner, 2026-10-11).

  • Admin notifications

    One bell with Personīgie / Administrācija tabs, and the admin icon menu. Built in Session 2.

  • Messages

    A side drawer from the right for quick replies from any page; /messages stays the full view. Group conversation = person and the whole team; the team speaks as the group.

  • Dates show the weekday

    Event rows read "Sv., 11. okt. 17:00".

  • Encourage, don't count

    Faces of who is coming instead of "0 apstiprināti"; sentence case, no capitals.

What I saw on the live site (10 October)
  • The brand has no fixed face. "EK" badge, spaced capitals, a gradient slogan; on a group page the logo takes the group's colour.
  • The site's purple is a category colour. Indigo buttons match the Music category; every page has a different main colour.

  • Cards are mostly photo; the useful facts are small.
  • Capital letters everywhere read as shouting.
  • Two navigations on a group (sidebar and tabs) around very little content.
  • Numbers that discourage: "0 apstiprināti", "1 Pasākumi atrasti".
  • Settings in the spotlight: language and theme switches in every header.
