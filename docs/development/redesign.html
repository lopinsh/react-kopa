Still open

Only real questions. Each has the default I would build if you say nothing.

  1. The compact filter area: A, B or C?

    Three ways to save space, from other sites (Discovery → "More compact"). Default: A, the row you asked for, made smaller.

  2. Bottom bar on a group page: which tab is marked?

    A group page belongs to no tab of its own. Default: the tab you came from stays marked (Atklāt from discovery, Grupas from Manas grupas), as phone apps do.

  3. Where does Iestatījumi live?

    The profile menu links to it, and light/dark is there. Default: the fifth page of your own area, last in its menu, like a group's Iestatījumi.

  4. The bell on phones

    A drop-down is cramped on a phone. Default: the bell opens the Paziņojumi page of your own area directly.

  5. The end of the group list

    Events end with "Rādīt nākamo nedēļu"; groups have no rule. Default: 24 cards, then "Rādīt vēl", no endless scrolling (Handbook: calm, not sticky).

  6. The place for someone we don't know

    Signed out, we know nothing. Default: start on "Visur" and ask "Kur tu esi?" once, above the results; keep the answer on the device; never guess from the internet address. Signed in: the profile city.

  7. When is a notification read?

    You said you would decide later. Default: when you tap it, as today.

  8. Reported people in admin

    Not designed yet. Default: later, as a second tab in Moderācija → Sūdzības.
